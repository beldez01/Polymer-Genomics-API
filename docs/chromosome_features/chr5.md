# Chromosome 5

> *A paradox of genomic architecture -- the fifth largest chromosome, yet one of the most gene-poor, harboring the engine of telomere immortality and a duplicated death trap at 5q13 that destroys motor neurons.*

## Physical Properties

- **Length**: ~181.5 Mb (GRCh38), representing ~5.8% of total genomic DNA. Fifth largest human autosome.
- **Centromere type**: Submetacentric. Denver Group B (chromosomes 4-5).
- **Arm sizes**: Short arm (5p) ~48 Mb; long arm (5q) ~134 Mb.
- **GC content**: ~39.5%, below the genome-wide average of 41%.
- **Gene density**: 923 protein-coding genes = ~5.1 genes/Mb, one of the lowest among autosomes. Extreme gene deserts at 5p15 (3.1 Mb gene-free) and 5q34 (5.0 Mb gene-free).

## Genomic Architecture

- **5q13 duplication architecture**: The SMA region contains a massive inverted duplication of ~500 kb, producing telomeric and centromeric copies of SMN, NAIP, and other genes. Human-specific -- chimpanzees have only a single copy.
- **Conserved noncoding regions**: Gene-poor regions display remarkable conservation with non-mammalian vertebrates (chicken, frog, fish), suggesting deep functional constraint.
- **Fragile sites**: FRA5A at 5p13 and FRA5B at 5q14.

## Notable Genes & Loci

- **SMN1/SMN2** (5q13.2): Differ by a single nucleotide (C-to-T in exon 7) causing ~90% of SMN2 transcripts to skip exon 7. Homozygous SMN1 deletion causes SMA, with severity inversely correlated to SMN2 copy number. Target of three FDA-approved therapies.
- **APC** (5q22.2): Canonical tumor suppressor and gatekeeper of colorectal cancer. Somatic APC mutations in ~80% of sporadic colorectal cancers -- the earliest known genetic event in colorectal tumorigenesis.
- **TERT** (5p15.33): Catalytic subunit of telomerase, mapping within ~2 Mb of the telomere itself. The TERT promoter contains three parallel G-quadruplexes.
- **Protocadherin cluster** (5q31): Alpha (15), beta (15), and gamma (22) protocadherins spanning ~800 kb. Stochastic combinatorial expression creates a neuronal "molecular barcode."
- **Cytokine cluster** (5q31): IL-3, IL-4, IL-5, IL-9, IL-13, and GM-CSF within ~500 kb -- master regulator of allergic/Th2 responses.
- **NR3C1** (5q31.3): Glucocorticoid receptor. Epigenetic regulation by early-life stress is one of the most replicated findings in behavioral epigenetics.
- **CDH9/CDH10 desert** (5p14.1): Contains the strongest GWAS signal for autism (rs4307059).

## Disease Associations

- **Spinal muscular atrophy**: ~1:10,000 births; leading genetic cause of infant death before gene therapy. 94% caused by homozygous SMN1 exon 7 deletion.
- **Cri du Chat (5p-)**: ~1:50,000. Characteristic "cat cry" mapped to a 640 kb region at 5p15.3. TERT hemizygosity causes mildly shortened telomeres.
- **Familial adenomatous polyposis**: ~1:10,000; 100% penetrance for colorectal cancer by age 40 without colectomy.
- **5q- MDS**: del(5q) accounts for 10-15% of MDS. Exquisitely sensitive to lenalidomide through synthetic lethality with haplodeficient CSNK1A1.

## Evolutionary History

- **Ancestral arrangement**: Human represents the ancestral state; the pericentric inversion is chimpanzee-specific.
- **5q13 duplication is human-specific**: The SMN2 duplication occurred after human-chimpanzee divergence. SMA is a uniquely human disease.
- **Deep noncoding conservation**: Some ultraconserved elements in 5p14 desert show 100% identity between human and chicken across >200 bp.

## Biophysical & Structural Features

- **TERT promoter G-quadruplexes**: Three parallel G4 structures act as a transcriptional silencer. TRF2 creates a long-range chromatin loop between the 5p telomere and the TERT locus.
- **5q13 palindromic structure**: The inverted duplication creates a thermodynamically unstable palindrome prone to replication-based rearrangements, explaining the high de novo SMA deletion rate.

## Epigenetic Landscape

- **Protocadherin LRES**: Coordinated silencing of >50 protocadherin genes via CpG island hypermethylation spanning ~800 kb -- one of the largest known long-range epigenetic silencing events.
- **NR3C1 epigenetic programming**: Childhood abuse permanently alters CpG methylation at the exon 1F promoter, changing HPA axis reactivity for life.
- **TERT paradox**: Partial methylation of the TERT promoter is associated with expression in cancer, while complete unmethylation = silencing -- contradicting standard dogma.

## Deep Cuts

- **SMA is a human-only disease**: No other species naturally develops SMA because the SMN1/SMN2 duplication is unique to humans.
- **The single-nucleotide catastrophe**: SMN1 and SMN2 differ at one functionally relevant nucleotide, yet this converts a life-sustaining gene into one producing 85-90% defective protein.
- **TERT at its own telomere**: If chromosome 5's telomere erodes, telomere position effect could silence TERT when it's needed most.
- **Lenalidomide synthetic lethality**: CSNK1A1 haploinsufficiency from del(5q) makes the remaining allele susceptible to cereblon-mediated degradation, selectively killing del(5q) clones.
- **The protocadherin barcode**: Each neuron stochastically expresses a unique protocadherin combination for self-avoidance -- the nervous system's answer to immunoglobulin diversity.
