# Chromosome 17

> *The evolutionary black sheep: the most rearranged chromosome in the primate lineage, the densest in cancer genes, and home to a 900-kb inversion that may have shaped the European mind.*

## Physical Properties

- **Length**: ~84.3 Mb (T2T-CHM13). ~2.5% of total nuclear DNA.
- **Centromere type**: Submetacentric. Denver Group E.
- **Arm sizes**: p arm ~24 Mb (extraordinarily gene-dense), q arm ~60 Mb.
- **GC content**: ~45.5%, third-highest autosome (after chr19 and chr22).
- **Gene density**: 1,100-1,200 protein-coding genes -- second most gene-dense chromosome, tied with or just behind chr19.
- **Segmental duplications**: Fourth-highest duplication density among autosomes.

## Genomic Architecture

- **Isochore structure**: Predominantly GC-rich H2/H3 isochores -- unusual for this size.
- **LCR architecture**: 17p11.2 and 17p12 harbor complex low-copy repeats mediating recurrent microdeletions/microduplications (CMT1A-REPs, SMS-REPs).
- **Evolutionary breakpoints**: 20 rearrangement breakpoints in human lineage vs only 3 in mouse -- the most dramatic interspecific contrast for any autosome. Nearly all co-localize with segmental duplications.

## Notable Genes & Loci

- **TP53** (17p13.1): "Guardian of the genome." Mutated in >50% of all human cancers. >100,000 publications. Germline mutations cause Li-Fraumeni syndrome.
- **BRCA1** (17q21.31): Essential for homologous recombination. 45-85% lifetime breast cancer risk. Three Ashkenazi founder mutations carried by ~2% of that population.
- **ERBB2/HER2** (17q12): Amplified in ~20% of breast cancers. Target of trastuzumab.
- **NF1** (17q11.2): ~300 kb with 57 exons. Contains three embedded genes within its introns (OMGP, EVI2B, EVI2A) transcribed from the opposite strand -- a "Russian nesting doll" arrangement.
- **PMP22** (17p12): 1.5-Mb duplication = CMT1A (most common inherited neuropathy). Reciprocal deletion = HNPP.
- **RAI1** (17p11.2): Deletion = Smith-Magenis syndrome (inverted melatonin rhythm). Duplication = Potocki-Lupski syndrome.
- **MAPT** (17q21.31): Tau protein. Neurofibrillary tangles in Alzheimer's and frontotemporal dementia.
- **HOXB cluster** (17q21.32): Nine homeobox genes. HOXB13 G84E confers prostate cancer susceptibility.
- **Growth hormone cluster** (17q22-q24): Five tandemly duplicated primate-specific genes.
- **PAFAH1B1/LIS1** (17p13.3): Haploinsufficiency causes lissencephaly (smooth brain).

## Disease Associations

- **Cancer**: The single most cancer-relevant autosome. TP53, BRCA1, HER2, NF1, RNF43, MAP2K4, RAD51C, RARA, TOP2A. Loss of 17p is among the most common cytogenetic events across cancers.
- **CMT1A/HNPP**: Reciprocal 1.5-Mb duplication/deletion.
- **Smith-Magenis/Potocki-Lupski**: Reciprocal NAHR-mediated disorders.
- **17q21.31 microdeletion (Koolen-de Vries)**: ~600-kb deletion within the H2 haplotype. Developmental delay, friendly personality.
- **NF1**: Incidence 1:3,000. Cafe-au-lait spots, neurofibromas, optic gliomas. ~50% de novo mutation rate.

## Evolutionary History

- **"Evolutionary black sheep"**: 20 breakpoints in human lineage vs 3 in mouse -- most dramatic contrast for any autosome. Yet interchromosomal synteny is perfectly preserved across mammals.
- **17q21.31 inversion polymorphism**: ~900-kb inversion containing MAPT. H2 (ancestral, inverted) found ~20% in Europeans, nearly absent elsewhere. Under positive selection -- associated with increased female fecundity.
- **"Seed and spread"**: Duplicons spread locally then redistributed by inversions during primate evolution, creating the complex LCR architecture underlying modern genomic disorders.
- **Paradox**: Interchromosomal stability with intrachromosomal instability -- unique to chr17.

## Biophysical & Structural Features

- **TP53 promoter G-quadruplex**: Well-characterized G4 regulating transcription. Small molecule stabilization can repress TP53.
- **Replication-transcription conflicts**: Extreme gene density creates elevated collision burden between replication and transcription machinery.
- **HOXB chromatin**: Bipartite 3D structure with sharp boundary between active and repressed domains.

## Epigenetic Landscape

- **TP53 methylation**: Promoter hypermethylation provides an alternative to mutation for p53 inactivation.
- **Growth hormone cluster**: Model for tissue-specific epigenetic activation. Locus control region drives histone acetylation in pituitary; placental genes silenced by methylation elsewhere.
- **H1/H2 replication timing**: The MAPT/17q21.31 region shows haplotype-dependent replication timing differences.
- **17p13 euchromatin**: One of the most consistently euchromatic regions in the genome across all ENCODE cell lines.

## Deep Cuts

- **H2 fertility paradox**: The H2 haplotype is under positive selection because carrier women have more children -- yet it predisposes to the 17q21.31 microdeletion causing intellectual disability. Selection favors the haplotype despite its pathogenic potential.
- **Genes within genes**: NF1 harbors OMGP, EVI2B, and EVI2A transcribed from the opposite strand inside its introns -- exceedingly rare architecture.
- **Smith-Magenis inverted clock**: The only known genetic syndrome with a fully inverted circadian melatonin pattern -- peak during daytime.
- **TP53 publication dominance**: >100,000 publications. Named "Molecule of the Year" by Science in 1993.
- **The CMT1A dosage mirror**: Three PMP22 copies = demyelinating neuropathy. One copy = pressure palsies. Two copies = the only acceptable dose.
- **Chromosome 17 defies the rule**: Despite being the second most gene-dense autosome, it has the highest intrachromosomal rearrangement rate -- explained by segmental duplication architecture, not gene content.
