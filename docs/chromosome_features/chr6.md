# Chromosome 6

> *The immunological chromosome -- a 7.6-megabase fortress of genetic diversity at 6p21 that has been under balancing selection for 31 million years, containing more disease-associated variation than the rest of the genome combined.*

## Physical Properties

- **Length**: ~170.8 Mb (GRCh38), representing 5.5-6.0% of total genomic DNA.
- **Centromere type**: Submetacentric. Denver Group C (chromosomes 6-12 + X).
- **Arm sizes**: Short arm (6p) ~60 Mb; long arm (6q) ~110 Mb.
- **GC content**: ~39.6% overall, but with extreme local variation: MHC Class III exceeds 50%, while gene deserts drop below 37%.
- **Gene density**: 1,557 protein-coding genes. The extended MHC (eMHC) reaches 68 total genes per Mb -- the single most gene-dense region in the entire human genome.

## Genomic Architecture

- **The MHC/HLA super-locus**: 7.6 Mb at 6p21.3 containing ~260 expressed genes among 523 total loci in five sub-regions. Most gene-dense and most polymorphic region in the genome.
- **Long-range LD**: Ancestral haplotypes (e.g., A1-B8-DR3) extend across 3+ Mb with little recombination, maintained by selection and suppressed recombination.
- **Structural DNA variation**: Different MHC haplotypes show distinct stacking energy, curvature profiles, and open chromatin patterns.
- **Fragile sites**: FRA6E (6q26) coincides with PARK2/parkin gene, contributing to deletions in both Parkinson disease and cancer.

## Notable Genes & Loci

- **HLA-A, -B, -C** (Class I MHC): Present intracellular peptides to CD8+ T cells. HLA-B is the most polymorphic locus in the genome (>4,000 alleles). 42,193 unique nucleotide variants across all 45 HLA genes.
- **HLA-DR, -DQ, -DP** (Class II MHC): Present extracellular peptides to CD4+ T cells. DQ heterodimers form in cis or trans, quadrupling diversity.
- **HLA Class III**: Densest gene region in the genome -- complement (C2, C4A, C4B), TNF-alpha, HSP70. C4 copy number varies 2-8 per diploid genome.
- **VEGFA** (6p21.3): Master regulator of angiogenesis. 4+ isoforms by alternative splicing. Target of bevacizumab.
- **PARK2/Parkin** (6q25-27): E3 ubiquitin ligase spanning 1.38 Mb. Mutations cause ~15% of familial PD. Overlaps FRA6E fragile site.
- **HFE** (6p22.2): Atypical MHC class I molecule regulating iron absorption. C282Y is ~1:200 in Northern Europeans.
- **HLA-G** (6p21.3): Non-classical MHC-I expressed on placental trophoblast. Suppresses maternal NK cell attack -- molecular key to mammalian pregnancy.

## Disease Associations

- **Type 1 diabetes**: MHC/HLA accounts for ~50% of genetic risk. DR3-DQ2 and DR4-DQ8 = risk; DR15-DQ6 = strongly protective (OR ~0.03).
- **Ankylosing spondylitis**: HLA-B27, OR >100 -- strongest HLA-disease association known.
- **Celiac disease**: DQ2/DQ8, near-obligate. >99% of patients carry one or both.
- **Schizophrenia**: Strongest GWAS signal driven by C4A copy number variation -- excessive synaptic pruning mechanism.
- **Hereditary hemochromatosis**: HFE C282Y homozygosity in ~1:200 Northern Europeans.
- **GWAS density**: 6p21 has the highest density of GWAS hits per megabase in the genome.

## Evolutionary History

- **Centromere repositioning**: Ancestral centromere at 6p22.1 relocated to current position 17-23 Mya. A human neocentromere was discovered at the exact ancestral position -- "centromere memory" persisting for 17+ million years.
- **MHC antiquity**: Recognizable organization appears with jawed vertebrates ~450 Mya. Shark MHC already contains class I and II genes.
- **Trans-species polymorphism**: HLA-DQB1 lineages maintained by balancing selection for at least 31 million years -- a human can share more HLA sequence with a macaque than with their neighbor.

## Biophysical & Structural Features

- **MHC structural polymorphism**: Complete haplotype sequences reveal variability in DNA stacking energy, curvature, and repeat composition between haplotypes.
- **G-quadruplex enrichment**: GC-rich MHC Class III has elevated G4-forming potential. G4 structures in HLA promoters may influence immune gene regulation.
- **Open chromatin density**: MHC Class III has the highest density of DNase I hypersensitive sites per Mb in the genome.

## Epigenetic Landscape

- **CIITA master regulation**: HLA expression controlled by CIITA, regulated by four promoters with cell-type-specific methylation. CIITA methylation = primary immune evasion mechanism in cancer.
- **6q24 imprinting**: PLAGL1 and HYMAI imprinted domain. Three mechanisms of neonatal diabetes: paternal UPD6 (41%), duplication (29%), maternal hypomethylation (30%).
- **Super-enhancers**: Extraordinary density of active enhancers in immune cells, with cell-type-specific activation across MHC sub-regions.

## Deep Cuts

- **Your HLA may be older than your species**: Two humans can differ more at HLA-DQB1 than a human and a baboon sharing the same ancestral lineage. Among the oldest known examples of balancing selection.
- **The autoimmune haplotype**: A1-B8-DR3 extends 3+ Mb and is associated with virtually every autoimmune disease. Carried by ~8% of Northern Europeans.
- **The centromere ghost**: A neocentromere jumped back to 6p22.1 -- where the ancestral centromere sat 17-23 Mya. "Centromere-forming potential" persisted across millions of years.
- **C4 and schizophrenia**: Each additional C4A copy increases risk ~1.27-fold. Excessive synaptic pruning during adolescence provides a molecular mechanism linking immunity to psychosis.
- **HLA-G and pregnancy**: Without HLA-G, mammalian viviparity as we know it would be immunologically impossible.
- **Olivia Farnsworth**: A 6p deletion produced a child who cannot feel pain, hunger, or the need for sleep -- perhaps the most dramatic microdeletion phenotype known.
