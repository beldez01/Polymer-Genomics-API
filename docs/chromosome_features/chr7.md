# Chromosome 7

> *The chromosome of voice, breath, and body plan -- where the gene for human speech sits alongside the blueprint for lung epithelial channels and the master regulators of embryonic patterning.*

## Physical Properties

- **Length**: ~158 Mb, representing 5.0-5.5% of total genomic DNA. First metacentric chromosome fully sequenced (Hillier et al. 2003).
- **Centromere type**: Submetacentric. Denver Group C. Contains two distinct alpha-satellite arrays (D7Z1 and D7Z2).
- **Arm sizes**: 7p ~58 Mb, 7q ~100 Mb.
- **Gene density**: ~1,000-1,150 protein-coding genes. The q22 band reaches 56 genes in a single megabase -- more than 5x the chromosome average.
- **Segmental duplications**: 8.2% of the chromosome, with marked p/q arm asymmetry.

## Genomic Architecture

- **Isochore heterogeneity**: Strong GC variation -- gene-dense/GC-rich segments enriched in Alu elements correlate with exonic density.
- **Fragile sites**: FRA7G and FRA7H are aphidicolin-induced. IMMP2L at FRA7 spans >800 kb and requires more than one cell cycle to transcribe.
- **Largest gene**: CNTNAP2 at 7q35-q36 spans 2.3 Mb -- ~1.6% of the entire chromosome.

## Notable Genes & Loci

- **CFTR** (7q31.2): Cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator. deltaF508 accounts for ~70-80% of CF alleles. Most common lethal autosomal recessive disease in Caucasians.
- **FOXP2** (7q31): Discovered via the KE family with severe verbal dyspraxia. Two human-specific amino acid changes are shared with Neanderthals.
- **HOXA cluster** (7p15): 11 homeobox genes in ~110 kb, expressed colinearly along the anterior-posterior body axis.
- **EGFR** (7p12): Amplified in ~40% of glioblastomas, mutated in ~15% of NSCLC. Gain of 7p12 in 71% of early squamous cell lung carcinomas.
- **SHH** (7q36): Sonic Hedgehog morphogen. Haploinsufficiency causes holoprosencephaly in humans but not mice -- a species difference in dosage sensitivity.
- **BRAF** (7q34): V600E is the iconic melanoma mutation. Within the 7q22-q34 commonly deleted region in myeloid neoplasms.

## Disease Associations

- **Cystic fibrosis**: >2,000 CFTR mutations catalogued. Carrier frequency ~1/25 in Europeans.
- **Williams-Beuren syndrome**: 1.5-1.8 Mb deletion at 7q11.23 removing 26-28 genes. Supravalvular aortic stenosis, hypersociability, visuospatial disability. Reciprocal duplication causes opposite phenotype: social anxiety.
- **Monosomy 7/del(7q)**: Among the most prognostically adverse cytogenetic abnormalities in myeloid neoplasms. SAMD9/SAMD9L germline mutations predispose to hereditary MDS with monosomy 7.
- **Cancer**: EGFR (glioblastoma, NSCLC), BRAF (melanoma), MET (gastric, lung). Trisomy 7 is the most common chromosomal gain in glioblastoma.

## Evolutionary History

- **Ancestral composition**: Derives from fusion of two ancestral mammalian chromosomes (7a and 7b/16p). Two major inversions shaped chr7 in the great ape lineage, all four breakpoints flanked by segmental duplications.
- **Marmoset segments**: Sequences from marmoset chromosome 2 were split across 7p22, 7q11, and 7q22 by these inversions.

## Biophysical & Structural Features

- **G-quadruplexes at fragile sites**: G4-forming motifs are enriched at common fragile sites on chr7, associated with ~70% of genes involved in lymphoid cancer rearrangements.
- **CNTNAP2 fragility**: At 2.3 Mb, structurally challenging for replication machinery, recurrently disrupted in neuropsychiatric disorders.

## Epigenetic Landscape

- **Imprinted genes**: GRB10 (7p12, maternally expressed), PEG10 (7q21.3, paternally expressed, retrotransposon-derived), MEST/PEG1 (7q32, paternally expressed).
- **HOXA methylation**: Differential methylation in cancer; HOXA10 hypermethylation associated with glioblastoma stem-cell signature.
- **Allele-specific replication**: Imprinted genes show asynchronous replication timing established in the germline.

## Deep Cuts

- **Williams syndrome and personality**: One of the most striking genotype-personality correlations -- hypersocial, musically gifted, yet profoundly disabled in visuospatial tasks. The reciprocal duplication produces social anxiety. Makes 7q11.23 the most informative CNV for genetics of sociality.
- **FOXP2 is not "the" language gene**: Better described as a gene for vocal motor learning shared with songbirds, bats, and cetaceans. Its "human-specific" changes are shared with Neanderthals.
- **CFTR heterozygote advantage**: deltaF508 reaches ~4% frequency in Europeans -- leading hypothesis is resistance to cholera/typhoid.
- **SHH dosage paradox**: Humans are haploinsufficient for SHH while mice require complete loss, demonstrating human development operates closer to morphogen sufficiency thresholds.
