# Chromosome 8

> *The first autosome sequenced from telomere to telomere -- whose vast regulatory desert orchestrates cancer at a distance, whose centromere reveals the geological record of primate evolution, and whose short arm carries an inversion shared by a quarter of humanity.*

## Physical Properties

- **Length**: 146,259,670 bp in the T2T assembly (Logsdon et al. 2021), including 3.3 Mb missing from GRCh38. ~4.5-5.0% of genomic DNA.
- **Centromere**: Submetacentric. 2.08 Mb alpha-satellite (D8Z2) array with a 73-kb hypomethylated CENP-A domain marking the functional kinetochore.
- **Denver Group**: C.
- **Gene count**: ~700 protein-coding genes. T2T resolved 61 protein-coding and 33 non-coding loci missing from GRCh38.
- **Base accuracy**: >99.99%, the gold standard for complete chromosome assembly.

## Genomic Architecture

- **8p23.1 inversion polymorphism**: 3.8-4.5 Mb segment exists in two orientations in ~26% of individuals. Flanked by olfactory receptor/defensin clusters (REPD/REPP). Can predispose to invdupdel(8p) rearrangements.
- **Beta-defensin CNV**: 644-kb copy-number-variable cluster (DEFB4, DEFB103, DEFB104). Copy number ranges 1-12 per haploid genome. Low copy number (<=3) confers 3x increased Crohn disease risk.
- **8q24 gene desert**: ~1.8 Mb nearly devoid of coding genes, yet one of the most important regulatory landscapes in the genome -- harbors more cancer GWAS risk SNPs per Mb than almost any other region.
- **Neocentromere**: 863-kb VNTR at 8q21.2 can function as a neocentromere -- one of the best-characterized in the human genome.

## Notable Genes & Loci

- **MYC** (8q24.21): Master transcriptional amplifier. Amplification/overexpression in >70% of human cancers. Regulated by distant enhancers in the gene desert.
- **PVT1** (8q24.21): lncRNA 55 kb downstream of MYC. Co-amplified in cancer; stabilizes MYC protein. Gain of Myc alone is insufficient for tumorigenesis in mice -- Pvt1 co-gain is required.
- **8q24 enhancer landscape**: GWAS SNPs in at least 5 independent risk regions for prostate, breast, colon, esophageal, ovarian, and pancreatic cancer. Tissue-specific long-range chromatin loops with MYC promoter.
- **FGFR1** (8p11.23): Rearrangements define 8p11 myeloproliferative syndrome with 17 known fusion partners. Rapid progression to AML.

## Disease Associations

- **Burkitt lymphoma**: t(8;14)(q24;q32) places MYC under IGH enhancer control. Described by Denis Burkitt in 1958; translocation identified in 1972.
- **Trisomy 8 mosaicism (Warkany syndrome)**: 1/25,000-50,000. Predisposes to Wilms tumor, MDS, and AML.
- **Trisomy 8 in AML**: Most common numerical abnormality, 10-20% of cases. Intermediate prognosis.
- **8p loss/8q gain in prostate cancer**: One of the most frequent structural abnormalities.

## Evolutionary History

- **First complete autosome**: First T2T autosome (2021), third completed centromere (after X and Y).
- **Centromere evolution**: Comparative assembly revealed layered, mirrored symmetry -- more ancient HOR repeats pushed to periphery while newer occupy functional center, like geological strata.
- **8q24 conservation paradox**: Gene desert deeply conserved across mammals despite being nearly devoid of protein-coding genes. Murine ortholog deletion has anti-cancer effects.

## Biophysical & Structural Features

- **G-quadruplexes**: T2T revealed significantly more G4 structures than GRCh38 had suggested -- collapsed in earlier assembly.
- **Centromeric methylation**: Broadly methylated alpha-satellite with a distinctive 73-kb hypomethylated CENP-A island.

## Epigenetic Landscape

- **8q24 tissue-specific enhancers**: H3K4me1 >> H3K4me3 (enhancer signature). Chromatin looping to MYC is tissue-specific: prostate risk SNPs loop to MYC in prostate but not breast, and vice versa.
- **PVT1 dual isoforms**: Linear PVT1 stabilizes MYC protein; circular PVT1 (circPVT1) has distinct functions in hematological malignancies.

## Deep Cuts

- **The 8q24 "desert" is an oasis**: One of the most transcriptionally active non-coding regions, producing multiple lncRNAs and miRNAs.
- **PVT1 is MYC's essential co-conspirator**: Mouse models show MYC gain alone is insufficient for tumorigenesis without PVT1.
- **The 8p23.1 inversion is invisible**: Present in ~26% of the population, this 4.5 Mb inversion is cytogenetically indistinguishable from the direct orientation.
- **Centromere as geological record**: The layered alpha-satellite structure is a molecular fossil record of primate evolution, readable like tree rings.
- **Defensin copy number determines Crohn risk**: One of the clearest examples of CNV directly modulating innate immune function and disease susceptibility.
