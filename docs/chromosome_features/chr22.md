# Chromosome 22

> *"The smallest shall be first" -- the first human chromosome fully sequenced, yet a hotspot for the most consequential structural rearrangements in medicine.*

## Physical Properties

- **Length**: ~50.8 Mb (actually slightly larger than chr21 despite higher number -- historical misnaming).
- **Centromere type**: Acrocentric, Group G. Short arm contains rDNA arrays shared with chr13, 14, 15, 21.
- **Arm sizes**: p arm ~16.6 Mb (rDNA/repetitive), q arm euchromatin ~33.4 Mb.
- **GC content**: 47.8% -- one of the most GC-rich chromosomes.
- **Gene density rank**: 2nd highest in the genome at ~17 genes/Mb, behind only chr19.
- **CpG island density**: Twice the genomic average.
- **Protein-coding genes**: ~500-600.

## Genomic Architecture

- **LCR22s**: The 22q11.2 region contains extraordinary Low Copy Repeats -- 26 different haplotypes of LCR22A alone, ranging 250 kb to >2,000 kb. Expanded specifically in the human lineage.
- **PATRRs**: Palindromic AT-rich repeats predisposing to the recurrent t(11;22) translocation.
- **rDNA clusters**: Short arm harbors tandem 45S rDNA arrays contributing to nucleolus formation.

## Notable Genes & Loci

- **BCR** (22q11.23): Breakpoint Cluster Region -- partner in the Philadelphia chromosome t(9;22). Breakpoint region is remarkably small (~5.8 kb).
- **NF2/Merlin** (22q12.2): Loss causes NF2 (bilateral vestibular schwannomas, meningiomas). Links actin cytoskeleton to cell membrane.
- **COMT** (22q11.21): The "warrior vs. worrier" gene. Val158Met modulates prefrontal dopamine. Hemizygous in 22q11.2 deletion carriers, contributing to 30x schizophrenia risk.
- **SHANK3** (22q13.33): Haploinsufficiency causes Phelan-McDermid syndrome (autism, absent speech).
- **IGL** (22q11.2): Immunoglobulin lambda light chain locus, ~1 Mb with 73-74 V-lambda genes.
- **EWSR1** (22q12.2): t(11;22) fusion with FLI1 = hallmark of Ewing sarcoma.
- **TBX1** (22q11.21): Primary driver of cardiac/craniofacial features in DiGeorge syndrome.

## Disease Associations

- **22q11.2 Deletion (DiGeorge/VCFS)**: Most common microdeletion (~1:4,000). >180 clinical features including conotruncal heart defects, immune deficiency, ~30x schizophrenia risk.
- **Philadelphia chromosome t(9;22)**: First cancer-associated translocation (1960). BCR-ABL1 in >95% of CML. Led to imatinib.
- **Cat Eye Syndrome**: Partial tetrasomy of 22pter-22q11.
- **Emanuel Syndrome**: der(22)t(11;22). Carriers face ~10% risk per pregnancy.
- **Phelan-McDermid (22q13.3 deletion)**: SHANK3 disruption causes ASD and absent speech.
- **Schizophrenia**: 22q11.2 deletion is the single strongest known genetic risk factor (25-30% develop it).

## Evolutionary History

- **Historical misnaming**: Actually physically larger than chr21. Numbering preserved to avoid confusion with "Trisomy 21."
- **LCR22 expansion**: Human-specific, post-dating human-chimpanzee divergence.
- **rDNA concerted evolution**: p-arm rDNA maintains within-species homogeneity through unequal crossing-over.

## Biophysical & Structural Features

- **G-quadruplexes and TAD boundaries**: G4-containing TAD boundaries on chr22 show distinct chromatin interaction patterns.
- **PATRR secondary structures**: Form cruciform structures in vivo, directly causing the recurrent t(11;22) -- DNA secondary structure directly causing disease.
- **Nuclear positioning**: Gene-dense, GC-rich chr22 localizes to nuclear interior.

## Epigenetic Landscape

- **22q11.2 deletion epigenetic effects**: Hemizygosity causes altered methylation at hundreds of distal loci -- a trans-epigenetic effect.
- **High CpG island density**: Outsized proportion of regulatory islands susceptible to aberrant methylation in cancer.

## Deep Cuts

- **First chromosome sequenced**: December 1999 by the Sanger Centre/Oklahoma/WashU/Keio. 11 gaps in the 33.4 Mb q-arm. Beat chr21 by a year.
- **Double translocation hotspot**: Participates in two independent recurrent translocations -- t(9;22) and t(11;22) -- caused by entirely different sequence features. No other small chromosome has this.
- **The COMT paradox**: In 22q11.2 deletion, a single remaining COMT allele determines dopamine phenotype absolutely -- no second allele to buffer the effect.
- **IGL somatic rearrangement**: chr22 is one of the most somatically rearranged chromosomes in healthy humans through V(D)J recombination in every B cell.
