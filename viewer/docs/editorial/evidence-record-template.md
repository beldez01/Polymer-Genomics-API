## Source verification

For each source record the access route, date, main-text passages actually read, supplement passages actually read, and inaccessible material. Distinguish abstract verification, targeted full-text inspection, and complete reading. Record corrections/retractions and later tests; an unsuccessful search is not proof that none exist.

## Recent literature and challenges
