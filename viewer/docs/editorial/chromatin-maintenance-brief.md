# Chromatin maintenance: brief and claim/source record

Date: 2026-11-11

Status: Initial sourced editorial draft; scientific review pending

## Brief

Central question: How do cells organize, alter, and restore chromatin while
preserving the capacity to change state?

Audience: Readers familiar with DNA and genes who need a coherent explanation
of chromatin activities, replication-associated restoration, and maintenance
methylation. Define molecular abbreviations and distinguish substrates.

The article introduces structure, machinery, replication, DNA methylation,
reader–writer relationships, material context, and assays. It includes a selected
activity/substrate table and an original schematic of parental/new histone
handling. It is not an exhaustive enzyme catalog or a clinical treatment guide.

## Interpretive material

The material-channel section is an editorial connection between quantities,
models, and experimental scales. It is not presented as a result of the papers
cited for individual mechanisms. No universal fractions of methylation-mediated
repression, phase transitions, or sequence-predicted chromatin states are claimed.

The tabled nucleosome-wrapping model recorded in
`~/Desktop/Research/topics/biophysics/nucleosome-wrapping-model-revision.md` is not
used to support the article or diagram. Earlier computational parameters and
predicted occupancies are not imported.
